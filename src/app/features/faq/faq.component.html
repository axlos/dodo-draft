What is DodoDraft and how does it work?

DodoDraft is an artificial intelligence-based text generator that creates unique job proposals tailored to your LinkedIn profile. It's commonly used to apply for freelance projects or job offers. You can choose to include or exclude your professional experiences, certifications, or languages in the context of your cover letter.

How does DodoDraft differ from other cover letter generators?

Have you ever spent hours crafting winning proposals for your clients or companies? DodoDraft's main goal is to save your time by creating unique job or project proposals that perfectly match your LinkedIn profile. Instead of generic proposals, we offer context-driven ones that align with the project and profile. Plus, you can exclude any irrelevant information such as unrelated job experiences or certifications.

Is my profile publicly visible?

Your profile is securely stored in our databases and is not shared with any external companies or clients for any reason.

Is my uploaded LinkedIn profile stored in any file system?

Your PDF profile is completely removed from our system once the information is extracted.
